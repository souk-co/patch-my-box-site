Patch My Box · a Souk

Got in? Fix it, and get paid.

An owner promises a reward to whoever breaks into their machine and repairs the whole thing. A Souk judges the proof, the repair and the conduct privately inside a sealed server, then commits a secret delay. If the exploit doesn't leak, the reward becomes a public debt the owner pays.

How it works

1 · OfferThe owner publishes patchmybox.txt (scope, reward, rules) and registers its hash on-chain. No reward is deposited up front.
2 · ClaimA researcher pays a small refundable fee on-chain, then opens a private case with the Souk: an attack.txt proof on their own domain and whole-machine repair evidence.
3 · JudgeA pinned judge in the sealed server rules proof, repair and conduct separately, checking the proof URL, the owner's leak source and the chain. The owner can pay to challenge within the reply window.
4 · Secret delayIf all three qualify, the Souk commits a release time only it can compute, longer when it is less sure. A leak or a new compromise during the delay forfeits the claim.
5 · DebtAt the release time the Souk reveals it; the reward becomes a public debt. The owner pays from their own wallet, earliest debts first.

The Souk never holds money. Only the Souk's key, held inside Intel TDX, can qualify, reveal or forfeit a claim; the owner pays; unpaid debts are visible to everyone.
